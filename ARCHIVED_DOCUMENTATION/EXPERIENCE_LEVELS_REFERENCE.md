## Increment Calculation Guide

### Experience Increment Formula

**Experience Increment = Applicant's Experience Level - Baseline Experience Level**

### Pattern
- **Each Level = 6 months more experience**
- **Level 1** starts at 0 months (range: 0-6)
- **Each subsequent level** adds 6 months
- **Level 31** is 15+ years (180+ months, maximum)

### Examples

#### Example 1: 2 years experience
```
Applicant Experience: 2 years = 24 months
Experience falls in Range: 2-2.5 years (24-30 months)
Experience Level: 5
Baseline for Teacher I: 1
Experience Increment: 5 - 1 = 4
Points: 4 (per rubric)
```

#### Example 2: 10 years experience
```
Applicant Experience: 10 years = 120 months
Experience falls in Range: 10-10.5 years (120-126 months)
Experience Level: 21
Baseline for Teacher I: 1
Experience Increment: 21 - 1 = 20
Points: 10 (maximum, 10+ increment rubric)
```

#### Example 3: 0 months (new teacher)
```
Applicant Experience: 0 months
Experience falls in Range: None to 6 months (0-6 months)
Experience Level: 1
Baseline for Teacher I: 1
Experience Increment: 1 - 1 = 0
Points: 0 (meets minimum)
```

```javascript
// Level 5 → 27 months (midpoint of 24-30 months = 2-2.5 years)

// Calculation:
// Increment = 5 - 1 = 4
// Points = 4 (based on increment rubric)
```

### Month Calculation Logic

**Representative Months by Level** (using range midpoints):
```
Each level = 6 months increment
Formula: (Level - 1) × 6 + 3
        = Midpoint of each 6-month range

Examples:
Level 1: (1-1)×6 + 3 = 3 months
Level 5: (5-1)×6 + 3 = 27 months
Level 21: (21-1)×6 + 3 = 123 months
Level 31: Special case = 180 months (15+)
```


## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Can't find experience level in dropdown
**Solution:**
1. Calculate total months of experience
2. Find the 6-month range where those months fall
3. Select the corresponding level from dropdown
4. System auto-calculates points

### Issue: Points seem incorrect for experience
**Solution:**
1. Verify correct level selected (check dropdown display)
2. Maximum experience points is 10 (even if increment is 20+)
3. Baseline is Level 1 for all applicants
4. Formula: Increment = Selected Level - 1

### Issue: Experience doesn't display correctly
**Solution:**
1. Make sure to select from dropdown (not type manually)
2. Selection must be one of the 31 levels
3. Click elsewhere to trigger calculation
4. Check hidden field has months value
