## Increment Calculation Guide

### Training Increment Formula

**Training Increment = Applicant's Training Level - Baseline Training Level**

### Pattern
- **Each Level = 8 hours more training**
- **Level 1** starts at 0 hours (range: 0-8)
- **Each subsequent level** adds 8 hours
- **Level 31** is 240+ hours (maximum)

### Examples

#### Example 1: 48 hours training
```
Applicant Training Hours: 48
Training Hours fall in Range: 48-56 hours
Training Level: 7
Baseline for Teacher I: 1
Training Increment: 7 - 1 = 6
Points: 6 (per rubric)
```

#### Example 2: 120 hours training
```
Applicant Training Hours: 120
Training Hours fall in Range: 120-128 hours
Training Level: 16
Baseline for Teacher I: 1
Training Increment: 16 - 1 = 15
Points: 10 (maximum, 10+ increment rubric)
```

#### Example 3: 32 hours training (DepEd Document Example)
```
Applicant Training Hours: 32
Training Hours fall in Range: 32-40 hours
Training Level: 5
Baseline for Teacher I: 1
Training Increment: 5 - 1 = 4
Points: 4 (per rubric)
```

```javascript
// Level 5 → 36 hours (midpoint of 32-40 range)

// Calculation:
// Increment = 5 - 1 = 4
// Points = 4 (based on increment rubric)
```

### Hour Calculation Logic

**Representative Hours by Level** (using range midpoints):
```
Level 1-31: (Starting at 0) + (Level - 1) × 8 + 4
            OR
            (Lower bound + Upper bound) / 2
```

**Examples:**
- Level 1: (0 + 8) / 2 = 4 hours
- Level 5: (32 + 40) / 2 = 36 hours
- Level 16: (120 + 128) / 2 = 124 hours
- Level 31: 240 hours (for 240+ range, use 240)

## Special Cases

### Training at Exact Boundary
```
Question: If teacher has exactly 40 hours?
Answer: 40 hours is the START of Level 6 range
Assigned Level: Level 6 (40-48 hours)
Why: Ranges are "From X" inclusive, "Less than Y" exclusive
```

### Training Hours Not in Any Range
```
Example: Teacher has 243 hours
Finding: Falls in Level 31 (240+)
Assignment: Level 31
Why: 240+ includes any hours at or above 240
```

### Training Exceeding Expected
```
Example: Teacher has 500 hours (highly unusual)
Finding: Still Level 31 (240+ range)
Increment: 31 - 1 = 30
Points: 10 (capped at maximum)
Why: System maxes out at 10 points regardless
```

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: User can't find their training level
**Solution:**
1. Calculate total training hours
2. Find the range where those hours fall (0-8, 8-16, 16-24, etc.)
3. Select the corresponding level from dropdown
4. System auto-calculates points

### Issue: Training hours don't seem to register
**Solution:**
1. Make sure to select from dropdown (not type manually)
2. Selection must be one of the 31 levels
3. Click elsewhere to trigger calculation
4. Check hidden field has hours value

### Issue: Points seem incorrect for training
**Solution:**
1. Verify correct level selected (check dropdown display)
2. Maximum training points is 10 (even if increment is 20+)
3. Baseline is Level 1 for all applicants
4. Formula: Increment = Selected Level - 1
