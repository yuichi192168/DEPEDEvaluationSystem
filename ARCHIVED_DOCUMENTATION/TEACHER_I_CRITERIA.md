# Teacher I Hiring Criteria Implementation Guide

## Overview
This document outlines the criteria and point system for evaluating Teacher I position applicants based on DepEd Order No. 007, s. 2023.

## Total Points System
**Maximum Total Points: 100**

### Criteria Breakdown

| Criteria | Maximum Points | Weight % | Description |
|----------|---|---|---|
| Education | 10 | 10% | Units and/or degree relevant to the position |
| Training | 10 | 10% | Training hours in Curriculum and Instruction |
| Experience | 10 | 10% | Teaching experience exceeding minimum requirements |
| PBET/LET/LEPT Rating | 10 | 10% | Professional Board/Licensure Examination Rating |
| PPST Classroom Observable Indicators (COI) | 35 | 35% | Classroom Observation/Demonstration Teaching |
| PPST Non-Classroom Observable Indicators (NCOI) | 25 | 25% | Teacher Reflection Form (TRF) |
| **TOTAL** | **100** | **100%** | |

## 1. EDUCATION, TRAINING, EXPERIENCE (ETE) Calculation

### 1.1 Education Increment Rubric
Based on Table 2.a from DepEd Order No. 007, s. 2023

**Points awarded based on number of increments from baseline:**

| Increments | Points |
|---|---|
| 10 or more increments | 10 |
| 8-9 increments | 8 |
| 6-7 increments | 6 |
| 4-5 increments | 4 |
| 2-3 increments | 2 |
| Less than 2 increments | 0 |

**Baseline for Teacher I:**
- Education: Level 6 (Bachelor's Degree)
- Applicant's actual qualification exceeding Level 6 = Increments

**Example:**
- Baseline: Bachelor's Degree (Level 6)
- Applicant: BSEd + 18 units towards Master's (Level 12)
- Increments: 12 - 6 = 6 increments
- Points awarded: 6 points

## 2. PBET/LET/LEPT Rating

**Formula:**
```
Points(PBET/LET/LEPT) = (PBET/LET/LEPT rating / 100) × WA(PBET/LET/LEPT)
```

Where:
- PBET/LET/LEPT rating = Applicant's final rating (0-100)
- WA = Weight Allocation = 10 points for Teacher I

**Example:**
- PBET rating = 82.75
- WA = 10
- Points = (82.75 / 100) × 10 = **8.275 points**

## 3. PPST Classroom Observable Indicators (COIs)

**Formula:**
```
Points(COI) = (COT rating / 30) × WA(COI)
```

Where:
- COT rating = Classroom Observation Tool rating (maximum 30)
- WA = Weight Allocation = 35 points for Teacher I

**Example:**
- COT rating = 20
- WA = 35
- Points = (20 / 30) × 35 = **23.333 points**

### COI Assessment Tools:
1. **COT-RSP Rubric** (Annex J) - Measures classroom performance against observable indicators
2. **Observation Notes Form** (Annex K) - Records specific observations and comments
3. **Rating Sheet** (Annex L) - Indicates rating/level attained
4. **IpEd Rating Sheet** (Annex L-I) - For IpEd teacher applicants
5. **Inter-Observer Agreement Form** (Annex M) - For inter-observer agreement exercise
6. **Inter-Observer Agreement Form** (Annex M-I) - For IpEd teachers

### PPST COI Strands/Indicators for Teacher I:

| Code | Indicator |
|---|---|
| 1.1.2 | Apply knowledge of content within and across curriculum teaching areas |
| 1.4.2 | Use a range of teaching strategies that enhance learner achievement in literacy and numeracy skills |
| 1.5.2 | Apply a range of teaching strategies to develop critical and creative thinking, as well as higher-order thinking skills |
| 4.1.2 | Plan, manage and implement developmentally sequenced teaching and learning processes to meet curriculum requirements and varied teaching contexts |
| 5.1.2 | Design, select, organize and use diagnostic, formative and summative assessment strategies consistent with curriculum requirements |

### Classroom Observation Protocols:

**Selection of Observers:**
- Trained COT assessors/observers (e.g., Master Teachers, Head Teachers, School Heads, Public School District Supervisors, Education Program Specialists/Supervisors)
- 2-3 observers shall participate
- Observers must not be related to applicant within the third degree of consanguinity
- Must review COT-RSP Rubric and forms before observation

**Observation Conduct:**
- Conducted in actual classroom setting with learners
- Entire class duration (45 minutes to 1 hour) recommended
- Can be shorter (15-minute minimum) if circumstances require
- Only Observation Notes Form brought during actual observation
- Rating Sheet accomplished individually after observation
- All observers meet to discuss and arrive at **collegial final rating**
- Final rating is not an average but based on objective, reasoned, and consensual judgment

## 4. PPST Non-Classroom Observable Indicators (NCOIs)

**Formula:**
```
Points(NCOI) = (TRF rating / 20) × WA(NCOI)
```

Where:
- TRF rating = Teacher Reflection Form total rating (maximum 20)
- WA = Weight Allocation = 25 points for Teacher I

**Example:**
- TRF rating = 14
- WA = 25
- Points = (14 / 20) × 25 = **17.5 points**

### NCOI Assessment Tools:
1. **Teacher Reflection Form** (Annex N) - Demonstrates non-classroom observable indicators through narratives
2. **Rubrics for Rating TRF** (Annex O) - Used to rate applicant's responses
3. **Inter-Evaluator Agreement Form** (Annex P) - For inter-evaluator agreement exercise

### PPST NCOI Strands/Indicators for Teacher I:

| Code | Indicator |
|---|---|
| 6.1.2 | Maintain learning environments that are responsive to community contexts |
| 6.3.2 | Review regularly personal teaching practice using existing laws and regulations that apply to the teaching profession and the responsibilities specified in the Code of Ethics for Professional Teachers |
| 7.2.2 | Adopt practices that uphold the dignity of teaching as a profession by exhibiting attitudes such as caring attitude, respect, and integrity |
| 7.3.2 | Participate in professional networks to share knowledge and to enhance experience |

### Teacher Reflection Form Administration Protocols:

**Format Options:**
- Online platform meeting (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.)
- In-person administration at designated location (preferably a room)
- Online via Google Form (with security measures enabled - Locked Mode)
- Email distribution (Links to individual documents managed and sent individually)
- 45 minutes to 1 hour duration recommended

**Security Measures:**
- Video/camera turned on if using online platform
- Proctor monitors answering of TRF
- Locked Mode enabled in Google Forms to prevent tab opening
- Long answers not required; quality of narrative counts

**Evaluation Process:**
- Each TRF evaluator rates individually
- All evaluators meet after rating to discuss
- Arrive at **collegial final rating**
- Final rating is not an average but based on objective, reasoned, and consensual judgment

## 5. Key Guidelines for ETE Qualifications

1. **Multiple Degrees/Units:**
   - Education units and/or degrees in multiple or different majors may be given corresponding points on a cumulative basis
   - Units or degrees must be relevant to the position applied for
   - Subjects completed must not be duplicated

2. **Digital/Online Training:**
   - Relevant training hours from digital/virtual/online learning may be considered
   - Subject to conditions in CSC Memorandum Circular (MC) No. 3, s. 2021

3. **Specialized Certifications:**
   - TESDA National Certification (NC) II and Trainers Methodology (TM) Certificate may be considered
   - Skills acquired must be relevant to work duties and Teacher I responsibilities

4. **Part-time Work Experience:**
   - Minimum four (4) hours per day
   - Certificate of Employment required with actual hours details
   - Months of relevant work shall be transmuted to equivalent months/years of experience

5. **Foreign Experience:**
   - Experience from abroad or outside Philippines may be considered
   - Must submit Certificate of Employment
   - Documents in non-English/Filipino must include complete English translation

6. **CSC RAOHRA:**
   - Applicable provisions under Rule VIII Part I to IV apply
   - In appreciation of relevant Education, Training, and Experience qualifications

## 6. Other Evaluative Assessments

### 6.1 Behavioural Events Interview (BEI)
- Conducted to gather additional information about teacher applicants
- Aspects include potential, character, and fitness

### 6.2 Skills or Work Sample Test (S/WST)
- May be conducted as necessary
- Especially for assessing applicants in certain tracks/strands/specializations

### 6.3 Other Evaluative Assessments
- As stipulated in Enclosure No. 1 of DepEd Order No. 007, s. 2023
- May include Ethics-Oriented and Personality Development Test

## 7. Baseline Qualification Standards for Teacher I

**Per CSC-approved Qualification Standards:**

### Minimum Requirements:
- **Education:** Bachelor of Secondary Education (BSEd) or Bachelor's degree plus 18 professional units in Education with appropriate major
- **Training:** None required
- **Experience:** None required

### Corresponding Baseline Levels:
- **Education:** Level 6 (Bachelor's Degree) or Level 12 (Bachelor's + 18 units towards Master's)
- **Training:** Level 1 (No training required)
- **Experience:** Level 1 (No experience required)

## 8. Implementation Notes

- Points must be calculated accurately using formulas provided
- All qualifications exceeding minimum requirements shall be included in increment computation
- Only qualifications relevant to the position shall be considered
- Meeting minimum (baseline) QS shall be given zero (0) points
- Formulas apply to both permanent and provisional/contractual applicants
- Inter-Observer/Inter-Evaluator Agreement Exercise is required for final rating determination
- Final ratings must be based on objective, reasoned, and consensual judgment
